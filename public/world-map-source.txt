Projection: Miller cylindrical, central meridian 0 degrees.
Canvas/viewBox: 0 0 900 440.
Longitude bounds: -180 to 180 degrees. Latitude bounds: -60 to 85 degrees.
Antarctica is omitted by the latitude cutoff.
Miller y(phi): 1.25 * ln(tan(pi / 4 + 0.4 * phi)), where phi is in radians.
x: ((longitude + 180) / 360) * 900.
y: ((MillerY(85) - MillerY(latitude)) / (MillerY(85) - MillerY(-60))) * 440.

Construction: Sample a regular lattice starting at (3, 3), spaced 6 SVG units.
Inverse-project each lattice point and retain points inside a source land polygon
and outside its interior holes. There are 3300 dots, radius 1.35 units,
fill #d6d3ca, with a transparent background.
No third-party map renderer or tracking script is embedded.

The corresponding marker projection is app/visitor-map-projection.ts.
